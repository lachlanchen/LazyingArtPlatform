Privacy

You can browse the products without an account. This hub uses no advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Images and styles come from this site. Our hosting infrastructure still processes ordinary connection and security information.

When you sign in

Optional sign-in uses the shared account service at chat.lazying.art. You enter your password there, not on this hub. The service shares your account identifier and display name so we can show your account page.

Essential, secure cookies protect the sign-in flow and remember your Platform session for up to 30 days. We store the session and its account-service credentials encrypted on our server, not in browser storage. Signing out removes the Platform session and requests revocation of its account-service access; it does not sign you out of every other app.

Your apps and LAC

Each app keeps its own workspace, permissions and purchases. LAC summaries are not connected on this hub yet. Signing in does not link a wallet, move coins or claim an award.

When you follow a link

The App Store, Google Play, Stripe, our shop, our blog and the Coin companion have their own privacy practices. They receive your visit when you open them. Payment and delivery details are entered with the relevant storefront or checkout, not on this hub.

Contact

For account or privacy questions, email hello@lazying.art. We receive the message and contact details you choose to send. Please do not send passwords, private keys, identity documents or payment-card details.
